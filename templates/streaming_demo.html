Algorithm: NormalizedDynamics with real-time incremental updates

Data: 6 simulated IoT sensors (temperature, humidity, pressure, CO₂, light, vibration)

Update Rate: ~10 points per second

Kernel Options:

  • Exponential: K = exp(-d / (2σ²)) - Linear distance decay. Works well empirically on biological trajectory data.
  • Gaussian: K = exp(-d² / (2σ²)) - Squared distance decay. Standard RBF kernel formulation.
  • Generalized: K = exp(-(d^p) / (2σ²)) - Generalized exponential family, tune p.
  • Student-t: K = (1 + d²/(νσ²))^(-(ν+1)/2) - Heavy-tailed kernel, tune ν.
  • Rational quadratic: K = (1 + d²/(2ασ²))^(-α) - Gaussian scale mixture, tune α.
